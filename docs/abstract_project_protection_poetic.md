In realms of code, where shadows softly creep,
And changes flow, in currents wide and deep,
A project breathes, a living, growing thing,
Yet fragile, too, where random touches sting.

Each action taken, a transaction's gleam,
A monadic pulse, a fleeting, vibrant dream.
But often, tasks, in half-light, incomplete,
Leave echoes faint, where perfect ends should meet.

For this, a shield, of ACLs, fine and true,
Granular guards, for all that's old and new.
No rogue command, no whisper out of place,
Shall mar the form, or leave a hidden trace.

A Quality System, Q, with watchful eye,
Surveys the State, where System G does lie.
A vibrant health, a truth it seeks to find,
The perfect form, for all of humankind.

And then, the Lattice, vast, a cosmic weave,
Where systems rise, and new connections cleave.
Each Rust-born Crate, a lattice in its soul,
A microcosm, striving for the whole.

Incompleteness reigns, in every single part,
A broken chord, a yearning, beating heart.
Yet in the Lattice, where all fragments blend,
A greater truth, a perfect form, transcends.

For tasks, though flawed, and data, ever new,
Find purpose deep, within this ordered view.
No single act, a perfect, finished grace,
But in the Lattice, all finds its true place.
