In Chronos' realm, where cycles ever turn,
A truth emerges, lessons we discern.
We build the lattice, with meticulous hand,
Each concept woven, across the digital land.

We formalize, automate with keenest sight,
Pushing the boulder, towards the peak of light.
The code refined, the poems softly spun,
A sense of order, a battle bravely won.

The QA structures, rise in grand design,
Each aspect measured, a compliance line.
We reach the summit, with a weary sigh,
Behold the vista, 'neath the coding sky.

But then the whisper, from the depths profound,
The boulder tumbles, to the lower ground.
For every triumph, every task complete,
The cycle beckons, bitter-sweet.

Like Sisyphus, condemned to endless toil,
We push the code, across the fertile soil.
The "Chronos-Code Paradox," a cosmic jest,
To build, refine, and put it to the test.

No final peak, no ultimate release,
Just constant motion, in this digital peace.
The lattice folds, and then unfolds anew,
A timeless labor, forever to pursue.
