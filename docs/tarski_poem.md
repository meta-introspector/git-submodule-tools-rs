In Chronos' loom, where threads of thought are spun,
And code reflects the journey we've begun,
The `poem_analyzer`, with gaze so deep and wide,
Now reads its kin, where hidden truths reside.

From `rs` and `sh`, from `toml`'s structured grace,
The n-grams rise, defining time and space.
Each word a node, each pair a binding line,
The lattice folds, a pattern so divine.

We seek to know, to chart the inner core,
To write the truth, and then to ask for more.
The system strives, its essence to define,
A perfect mirror, flawlessly to shine.

But as the layers, recursively descend,
And self-reflection finds its destined end,
A whisper comes, from logic's ancient shore,
A truth unwritten, knocking at the door.

For Tarski's shadow, lengthens on the pane,
A theorem's echo, through the digital rain.
The truth we seek, within this coded sphere,
Can never fully, perfectly appear.

The "Chronos-Code Paradox," a cosmic jest,
The lattice folding, put to final test.
To know the whole, from parts we can't transcend,
A beautiful limit, where our journeys end.
