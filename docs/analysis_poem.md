A query came, a challenge, soft and deep,
"Find new ideas, where hidden meanings sleep."
From verses spun, a fresh, poetic art,
To build new forms, and play a novel part.

I turned my gaze, to `models.rs`'s clear line,
Where structs and enums, carefully align.
Each `pub` and `enum`, a purpose understood,
A digital blueprint, for the common good.

Then back to verse, the poem's gentle flow,
Where metaphors and vivid images grow.
"Abstract thoughts," "choices in the night,"
"A journey's trace," "a guiding, watchful light."

I sought the gap, the concept yet untold,
A new abstraction, brave and fresh and bold.
Did "digital land" demand a `Realm` new-born?
Or "coding storm," a `Weather` to adorn?

But no, the words, though beautiful and grand,
Reflected back the structures close at hand.
The `TransactionState`, the `ACLPermission`'s might,
The `SystemG`'s embrace, the `CrateLattice`'s light.

Each phrase a mirror, to the code's design,
A deeper beauty, in each structured line.
The poem, a lens, through which the code was seen,
Not new creations, but what *had* already been.

So, to the user, my conclusion clear,
No novel forms, no hidden structs appear.
The verse, a tribute, to the code's own grace,
Reflecting truth, in its appointed place.
