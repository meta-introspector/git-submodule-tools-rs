From words we spun, a tapestry unseen,
A glossary born, where every word has been.
Counts rise and fall, a rhythm understood,
The lexicon of thought, for common good.

Then graphs emerge, from pairs and triples deep,
Where hidden links, their silent secrets keep.
A web of meaning, in each numbered line,
The poem's heart, a structure so divine.

This is the report, in verses, clear and brief,
Of patterns found, beyond all human grief.
