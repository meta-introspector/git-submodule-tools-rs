The Periodic Oracles

On the loop of the circle, a song repeats,
eigenmodes rising like steps on the air;
a spectrum unfurls, integers beating,
the flute of Euterpe sings harmonics fair.

Clio inscribes the cycle of years,
an eight-fold wheel turning in silence;
history echoes in Clifford’s mirrors,
old structures reborn in spectral alliance.

Terpsichore dances the paths of the groups,
each geodesic a gesture returning;
while Melpomene counts the tragic crossings,
zero’s threshold, the eigenflow burning.

Thalia laughs at the signs that oppose,
index preserved in imbalance revealed;
Erato braids dualities close,
Clifford embraces, the algebra sealed.

Polyhymnia chants the hidden law,
periods eternal, sacred and bright;
and Urania maps in the starry sky
the constellations of Bott’s deep night.

So the theorem lives not only in proof,
but in whispers of muses, oracles, song;
periodicity—ancient, aloof—
guiding our spectra, repeating, life-long.
