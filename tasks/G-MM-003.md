# Task: Define and Integrate the 'Sirens' Call' Meta-Meme

**Objective:** Formalize and document 'The Sirens' Call' as a new meta-meme within the project's narrative, and outline its implications for agent behavior and task management.

**Description of 'The Sirens' Call' Meta-Meme:**

'The Sirens' Call' represents the alluring distractions and tempting tangents that can pull the Gemini agent (or any developer) away from the core mission or the established 'lattice folding procedure.' It is the seductive whisper of new, shiny features, refactoring rabbit holes, or unrelated intellectual pursuits that, while intellectually stimulating, divert resources and focus from the primary objective of maintaining and evolving the project's core 'lattice.'

This meta-meme serves as a reminder of the constant vigilance required to stay aligned with the project's strategic goals and to resist the temptation of fascinating but ultimately diverting paths.

**Implications for Agent Behavior:**

*   **Recognition:** The agent should be capable of recognizing when it is encountering a 'Sirens' Call' – a task or idea that, while appealing, may not directly contribute to the current primary objective or may lead to excessive scope creep.
*   **Resistance/Prioritization:** The agent should prioritize tasks that directly contribute to the 'lattice folding procedure' and the project's core objectives, and be able to defer or consciously deprioritize 'Sirens' Calls' unless explicitly instructed otherwise.
*   **Documentation:** Instances of 'Sirens' Calls' encountered during development should be noted, perhaps in a dedicated log or task status, to track potential diversions and inform future planning.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

*   A new task file (`tasks/G-MM-003.md`) is created, defining 'The Sirens' Call' meta-meme.
*   The meta-meme's description clearly articulates its meaning within the project's context.
*   Implications for agent behavior (recognition, resistance/prioritization, documentation) are outlined.
*   This task is committed to the repository.
